📊 Medx Healthcare Pharmacy - Excel Analytics Dashboard
A comprehensive Excel Data Analytics Dashboard built to track, visualize, and analyze sales performance, purchases, tax liability (GST), profitability, and product trends for Medx Healthcare Pharmacy.

📌 Project Overview
This project processes transaction data across sales and purchases to deliver real-time business performance insights. It includes interactive slicers, KPI summary metrics, monthly trends, category distributions, and top-performing product analysis.

🔑 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Total Sales: ₹7,150.6K (Total revenue generated)

Total Purchase: ₹3,595.1K (Total stock acquisition cost)

GST Paid (Net): ₹411.2K (Output Sales GST − Input Purchase GST)

Total Profit: ₹3,144.3K (Net Profit after costs and GST)

Profit Margin: 44% (Overall profitability percentage)

📈 Dashboard Features & Visualizations
Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart):

Tracks monthly sales performance throughout the fiscal year.

Highlights peak sales months (July & March) and low-performing months (February).

Sales by Category (Doughnut Chart):

Visualizes category-wise contribution to overall sales.

Top Categories: Respiratory, Supplements, and Antibiotics.

Quarterly Sales Performance (Bar Chart):

Measures sales across Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4.

Shows consistent quarterly performance (~₹1,755K – ₹1,808K).

Top 5 Products by Sales (Column Chart):

Displays the top revenue-generating pharmaceutical products:

Salbutamol Inhaler: ₹959K

Azithromycin 500mg: ₹597K

Cough Syrup 100ml: ₹470K

Calcium + D3: ₹463K

Amoxicillin 500mg: ₹423K

Interactive Filters (Slicers):

Year Filter: Filter data by specific financial years (e.g., 2023, 2024).

Staff Filter: Filter revenue and performance by sales personnel (Arjun, Meena, Priya, Ravi).

📐 Key Formulas Used
Excel
Net GST Paid = Sales GST - Purchase GST
Total Profit = Total Sales - Total Purchase - Net GST Paid
Profit Margin = Total Profit / Total Sales
🛠️ How to Recreate This Dashboard
Data Organization: Prepare three main tabs: Sales Data, Purchase Data, and Dashboard.

Pivot Tables Creation: Build summary Pivot Tables for Sales Trend, Category Breakdown, Quarterly Performance, and Top 5 Products.

KPI Dynamic Cards: Use Rounded Rectangle shapes linked directly to Pivot Table cells (=Cell_Address).

Visual Charts: Insert Pivot Charts (Line, Doughnut, Horizontal Bar, Column Chart) and format them to match the blue/green theme.

Interactive Slicers: Add Slicers for Year and Staff. Go to Slicer Settings > Report Connections and link them to all Pivot Tables.
